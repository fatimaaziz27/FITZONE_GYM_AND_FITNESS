# FitZone Gym & Fitness

A modern and responsive **gym and fitness website** built using **HTML5 and CSS3**.

FitZone is designed to provide a clean, attractive, and user-friendly online presence for a modern fitness center, featuring workout programs, expert trainers, membership pricing, and contact information.

## Features

* Responsive gym website design
* Modern navigation bar
* Hero section with call-to-action buttons
* About Us section
* Why Choose FitZone section
* Workout programs
* Expert trainers section
* Membership pricing
* Contact section
* Interactive hover effects
* Responsive cards and layouts
* Modern fitness-themed design

## Website Sections

### Home

A visually engaging hero section introducing FitZone with a strong fitness message and call-to-action buttons.

### About Us

Introduces FitZone and explains its focus on helping people become stronger, healthier, and more confident.

### Why Choose FitZone?

Highlights the main benefits of joining the gym, including:

* Modern Equipment
* Expert Trainers
* Flexible Hours

### Trainers

Showcases professional trainers with dedicated trainer cards and a fitness-focused design.

### Membership Pricing

Provides different membership plans:

* **Basic — $19 / Month**
* **Pro — $39 / Month**
* **Elite — $69 / Month**

The Pro plan is highlighted as the **Most Popular** option.

### Contact

Provides gym contact information and a contact form for users who want to get in touch.
